From Edit to Final Mix

We can handle the complete audio post-production workflow and keep the creative direction consistent from the first dialogue edit to the final mix.

  • Full audio post-production

    Dialogue editing, sound design, Foley, ambience and the mix, in one studio.

  • Direct collaboration

    You talk directly with the people working on your sound.

  • Consistent creative direction

    The same ears on your film from the first edit to the final mix.

  • Remote workflow

    Send picture and files from anywhere, and review the work online.

  • Stereo & Dolby 5.1

    Final mixes delivered in stereo and 5.1 surround.

How It Works

  1. 01

    We Discuss the Film

    We talk about your film, creative references, goals, timeline and project details.

  2. 02

    You Send the Materials

    You send the picture lock and the necessary files we discussed.

  3. 03

    Sound Post

    We work on the dialogue, sound design, Foley, ambience and mix.

  4. 04

    Review & Final Delivery

    You review the work, we make the agreed revisions, and deliver the final mixes.

About Deverx Sound Studio

Deverx Sound Studio is a film audio post-production studio founded by Dalel Savarin and Denis Plakhetko. The studio is led by Denis, a sound designer and re-recording mixer.

Based in Dallas, Texas, we work remotely with filmmakers and production teams worldwide.
