What you’ll learn

01 Ear & craft

Training your ear to hear sound that isn’t there yet. How to stylize sound. How to think like a sound designer.

02 Tools

Choosing a DAW and the trade-offs of each. Plugin types, what they actually do, and my go-to set. Headphones and studio gear — what to buy and why.

03 The work

How to approach a project properly. The technical side of getting the sound you hear in your head. Working with music and voice (processing, not composing). Mixing & mastering.

04 What you leave with

A portfolio built around you, detailed reviews of your work, a real understanding of how the industry runs — and genuine confidence with sound.
