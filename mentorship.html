What You’ll Learn

Ear & Craft

Train your ear to hear the sound that isn’t there yet. Learn how to shape and stylize sound, make intentional creative choices, and think like a sound designer.

Tools

Choose the right DAW for your workflow and understand the strengths and trade-offs of each. Learn which plugins actually matter, how to use them effectively, and what to look for when choosing headphones and studio gear.

The Work

Learn how to take a project from first idea to final mix — turning the sound you imagine into a finished soundtrack. We’ll cover dialogue and voice processing, working with music, sound design workflows, and the technical decisions behind a professional mix.

What You’ll Leave With

A portfolio-ready project, detailed feedback on your work, a practical understanding of professional audio post-production, and the confidence to approach sound with intention. Top students may also be considered for paid work on future Deverx projects.
